Husky A200 reverse odometry anomaly
Technical support request prepared 7 September 2026

SUMMARY
Please help diagnose a direction-dependent wheel-travel feedback anomaly on a Husky A200 running ROS 1 Noetic. During commanded straight reverse, the operator observes no noticeable physical heading change, but wheel odometry reports substantial positive yaw. The fused odometry follows it, causing a Unity panoramic viewer to rotate incorrectly. Right-side accumulated travel nearly stops decreasing, or increases slightly, while both reported side speeds remain negative.

This report does not establish a defective encoder or a confirmed firmware bug. The observation of physical heading is visual, not an independent instrumented measurement. No raw serial packet capture or encoder electrical measurements are available.

USER OBSERVATIONS AND REPRODUCTION
The operator reports forward travel is visually satisfactory, whereas reverse produces persistent leftward apparent camera/view drift. The operator reports the chassis itself does not visibly turn. Disabling the Unity odometry-based panorama correction removes this apparent drift but breaks robot-forward alignment when the robot turns.
Unity uses the orientation quaternion in /odometry/filtered to compute relative yaw and rotate the panorama; the diagnostic yaw-rate conversion from radians/s to degrees/s is for display only. The problem is therefore investigated at the upstream motion/feedback layer.
The attached bag files contain reverse commands and stationary periods, with no positive linear commands captured. They cannot independently establish normal forward operation or low-speed behavior. Those conditions should not be inferred from these recordings.

SOURCE REVIEW AND LIMITS
The upstream 0.6.11 driver logs decoded travel before updating joint positions. It rejects absolute per-update position deltas of 1 rad or more. No rejection messages were captured in the debug reproduction. Normal 10 Hz operation at 6.06 rad/s gives roughly 0.61 rad per cycle, below that threshold. We have not patched the driver or changed this threshold.
The host parser reads left/right travel from the same encoder message structure using indexed fields. No confirmed direction-specific parser defect has been demonstrated. Identifying the installed binary's exact build/patch provenance may still be necessary.

REQUESTED SUPPORT ACTIONS
1. Check for known A200 reverse encoder-travel/firmware issues matching this evidence and advise how to obtain the installed MCU firmware identifier.
2. Specify safe checks for the right-side encoder, harness and counting channels, including whether a technician inspection is recommended.
3. Advise how to capture raw encoder ticks or serial payloads through a supported method, without opening a second competing connection to /dev/prolific.
4. Confirm the correct wheel radius multipliers for this robot and how to restore them persistently if 0.5 is incorrect.
5. Confirm whether an IMU was supplied on this unit and how to restore its ROS 1 driver if present.
6. Recommend a repair and validation procedure that preserves correct real turn tracking; we do not want to hide the defect by freezing reverse heading or substituting commanded turns for measured turns.
